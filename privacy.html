Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Pickle Plate ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our iOS application (the "App").

Information We Collect

We aim to collect as little personal information as possible. Specifically:

  • Dietary preferences and allergens. Information you enter about your diet and allergens is stored on your device and used to generate recommendations.
  • Menu photos. Photos you capture of menus are processed to extract text. Images may be sent to our AI processing provider to generate recommendations and are not retained after processing.
  • Diagnostic data. If you opt in via iOS settings, Apple may share anonymous crash and usage data with us to help improve the App.

How We Use Information

Information is used solely to operate the App: reading menus, generating dish recommendations based on your preferences, and improving reliability.

Third-Party Services

Pickle Plate uses third-party AI services to process menu images and generate recommendations. These providers process the data on our behalf and are contractually restricted from using it for other purposes.

Data Retention

Your dietary profile and scan history are stored locally on your device. You can clear them at any time from within the App or by deleting the App.

Children's Privacy

Pickle Plate is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your Rights

Because we do not maintain personal accounts or store identifiable user data on our servers, you control your information by managing it directly within the App.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above.

Contact

Questions? Reach us at support@pickleplate.app.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Pickle Plate available on Android?

Not at this time. Pickle Plate is currently iPhone-only.

Does Pickle Plate work offline?

An internet connection is required to scan and analyze menus, since recommendations are generated by an AI service.

Can I trust the recommendations if I have a severe allergy?

Pickle Plate is a helpful tool, but it is not a substitute for talking to restaurant staff. Always confirm ingredients and preparation directly with the restaurant if you have a serious allergy. See our Terms of Service for more details.

How is my data used?

See our Privacy Policy for full details. In short: your dietary profile stays on your device, and menu photos are processed only to generate recommendations.

How do I delete my data?

You can clear your profile and scan history from within the App's settings, or by deleting the App from your iPhone.

Reporting a Problem

If something isn't working right, please email us with:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • The version of Pickle Plate you're using (found in Settings)
  • A description of what happened
